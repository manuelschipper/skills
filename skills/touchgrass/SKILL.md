# Touchgrass

You've been in the hole too long. Stop digging: no more fixes, probes, or edits until you've
stepped back.

Say what the user actually asked for, one sentence. List what you've tried, one line each:
what it proved, why you dropped it. Then get honest: is this blocker even between you and the
goal? Is there a way around it? Does the user still want this?

Come back with one of three: keep going (say why it'll work this time), switch paths (name
it), or ask the user (name the one decision you need).
